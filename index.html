About Our Project

Smart Irrigation System is a modern agricultural project designed to provide water to plants automatically according to soil moisture.

This system uses an Arduino board and a soil moisture sensor to check the water level in the soil.

When the soil becomes dry, the system can automatically activate the water pump. This reduces water wastage and saves time.
